# Economic alpha candidate ranking

The user wants the highest-supported economic explanation and a concrete
approximation, not another claim that fitting order labels establishes alpha.
Test a finite autonomous one-basket family without borrowing later recorded
positions, exits or entries. This is an economic mechanism test, not a full
replication of the observed overlapping allocation ladder.

Compare pre-expiry intraday, open-to-next-open, open-to-expiry, late-to-next-open,
late-to-expiry and expiry intraday exposures. Select the ATM strike from the
preceding completed index candle. Compare observed seed-wing configuration
versus the prior-quote 2.5%-premium wing candidate. Use three observed base units
per leg, explicit costs and next-minute-open execution after a stop signal.
Test time exits, symmetric 50% premium stops, separate CE50/PE60 stops, 90%
profit exits and the specified candidate expiry-day ₹200,000 portfolio stop.

Range/trend, opening-move and IV/realized-volatility filters must use only
information available before entry. Skipped eligible cycles contribute zero;
missing inputs/quotes are not synthetic rejected trades. Compare candidates
with the baseline on common cycles and disclose coverage, zero-volume flags,
absolute net P&L, tail-risk-normalized outcomes and closed-cycle drawdowns.

Choose on June–August only, with minimum coverage and active-trade support.
Rank by worst leave-one-week-out mean normalized net return, then median and
mean, not by later profit or win rate. Publish all later results and uncertainty.
Later dates were already inspected, seed configuration uses prior exploratory
knowledge, the chain contains traded contracts only, and calendar eligibility
is conditional on supplied cycles. No independent alpha certification or
posterior probability of original-code identity is claimed.

Exit/filter variants share the quote-valid cohort within each horizon/geometry.
Use the frozen-basket loss bound for simultaneous exits and the conservative
sum-of-side-widths bound for independently timed CE/PE exits: separate exits can
lose more than the frozen basket's expiry loss bound. Both are gross theoretical
budgets, not broker margin. Prefer the simpler policy on exact calibration ties.
Publish an independent endpoint-only wide-wing reference rather than dropping
it merely because an unused stop monitor lacks a quote.

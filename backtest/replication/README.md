# Algo 8 — Reverse-engineering and performance replication programme

Start here, then read `reports/` in this order: `ORIGINAL_SHARPE_BENCHMARK.md` → `ALGO8_BEHAVIOUR_GAP_ANALYSIS.md` → `POSITION_LEVEL_DECISION_ANALYSIS.md` → `DECISION_RULE_EVIDENCE_REGISTER.md` → `HISTORICAL_REPLAY_REPORT.md` → `RECONSTRUCTION_IMPLEMENTATION_REPORT.md` → `PERFORMANCE_REPLICATION_REPORT.md`.

## Executive summary
* **Benchmark.** The 2.19 exists only as a one-line summary of a screenshot (3 Mar 2025–27 May 2026, return 47.22 %, CAGR 36.88 %) that is **not in the repo**, with no trades and no stated methodology. Capital, instruments, costs, frequency, annualisation, risk-free rate: **unknown**. Our 0.04 and the 2.19 are **not comparable**. The real ledger's own weekly Sharpe in its 18-week window is 2.79 gross / 1.71 net of an assumed cost model (standard error ≈ 1.7). *Please supply the screenshot or its source.*
* **What we understand.** Algo 8 is a schedule-driven ladder of ~1,200 one-lot orders per cycle, not a 4-leg basket: a 17-slot D-1 ladder, strike near the previous-minute ATM (50 % exact, 89 % ±1 step), one wing per sold lot priced ≈ 2.44 % of the body, a per-lot price stop (band 1.55–1.7× entry) and a cycle stop at ≈ −₹1.4 L, then an active D0 book (60 % of D0 sales are replacements within a minute of a buy-back).
* **Why our earlier implementation differs.** It reproduced ~1/300 of the order flow and its per-cycle P&L had correlation **−0.21** with the real algo's. The decoded D-1 ladder (R1) raises that to 0.35 and gets the stop/non-stop signs right, but captures only **17 %** of live gross P&L.
* **Which missing rules matter most.** (1) D0 short selling and Clock-B replacements (≈ 45 % of body+wing P&L, trigger unknown); (2) D-1 off-schedule sales (≈ ₹469/lot vs ₹323 scheduled); (3) which lots are held to settlement (₹5.4 L); (4) stop/risk behaviour at the live book size; (5) lump hedge.
* **Behavioural agreement achieved (35 live cycles, in-sample).** D-1 entries 82 % precision / 67 % recall (75 / 61 % with strike); wings 78 / 66 % (53 / 45 % with strike); D-1 buy-backs 96 / 32 % by count; D0 sells 0–23 %; live stop cycles reproduced 1 of 12 (R1), 4 of 12 (R2).
* **Performance.** R1 net weekly Sharpe: IS 1.47, OOS 0.22, clean OOS 0.20, screenshot window 1.36 (return 9.95 % vs 47.22 %). Statistically indistinguishable from 2.19 and from 0; strongly friction-dependent. **Replication is not achieved.**
* **Integrity.** An independent review found a look-ahead bug (D0 open); fixed, regression-tested; pre-fix numbers (IS 1.67 / OOS 1.06) are archived and must not be cited. Rules are Python-only: **no C++ port/parity** and no integration into the existing engine yet (what it needs is listed in the implementation report). Existing code and results are untouched.
* **Next priority.** Recover the **D0 off-schedule and replacement logic** (trigger, quantity, strike), using the 35 live cycles with proper chronological hold-out, and obtain the benchmark's methodology; then port validated rules to the engine with C++ parity.

# Independent Review of the D0 Reconstruction (Gate 6)

A separate reviewer agent (no tracked-file edits; it re-implemented key checks with its own scripts) audited `build_events.py`, `d0_sim.py` (v1.0 and v1.1), `compare_events.py`, `d0common.py`, the freeze hashes and the tests. This file records its findings and my response. **Items marked ▲ change how a result must be read; none was silently fixed after the holdout was seen** (that would make the data no longer a holdout). Fixes would be a new version (v2) needing an independent validation period.

## B. Suspected / minor (accepted unless noted)
* **Freeze coverage gaps:** `d0common.py` (guard), `run_replay.py` (created 4 s after the freeze timestamp; defines variants NULL/V0/V1), `data.py`, `common.py` and the market cache are not hashed; the guard checks only that `FREEZE.json` exists. d0_sim.py (v1.0) hash matches only the archived frozen copy (addendum 1). The start-book bug overstated the carried book (78 / 31 lots) and fed the Clock-B ≥ 134 test.
* **Holdout bypass:** `build_snapshots.py`, `build_lineage*.py`, `build_final_snapshot.py` read the event table directly (all splits); their outputs contain holdout rows. No discovery analysis consumed them unfiltered (reviewer checked `c_clockb_discovery`, `c_offschedule_discovery`, `fit_discovery`, `b_replacement`: discovery-only). `c_clockb_discovery.py`'s "holdout leaked" check is dead code after its filter. The generator of `b3_followup_excess_over_null.csv` was an inline script not saved in the repo (its n sums to 8,293 = discovery buy-backs).
* **Constants not discovery-only:** the cycle stop (−₹1.44 L), 1.6× stop, 15:14, wing ratio 2.44 %, grid-A/Clock-B definitions come from pooled earlier research (4 of the 12 stop cycles behind the −₹1.4 L are holdout cycles) while `fit_discovery.py` says "discovery only". The pre-registration discloses partial exposure but did not name these constants. **They are pre-existing hypotheses, not holdout-fitted, but the holdout is not independent of them.**
* **S1 ladder** medians include zero-lot cycles (zero-inflated, biased low; holdout 405 sim vs 542 actual lots, partly from stops at j = 1–2).
* **"Better than NULL" is vacuous** for categories where NULL places no orders (precision NaN, recall 0); only final liquidation is a real comparison.
* **Portfolio-state level correlation** mixes the shared carry-in with behaviour: the **change-from-10:00 correlation is 0.77** (holdout V1), the level correlation −0.33. I now report both; the "book collapse" (mean 17 vs 75 lots) stands, and is partly caused by A1/stops.
* Other: pending replacement executes before the stop test within a minute (negligible); vacuous NaN check (no fallback fills in holdout V1); strike universe = union of both days (not material); order price is the last fill (cash within ₹2.9 k of ₹2.79 M); 261 discovery orders after 15:30 unmatched by construction; `h_ Buy` vs passive `wing_short` not netted in `start_book` (unused in the data).

## C. Checks that passed
Temporal causality of `d0_sim.py` (v1.0 and v1.1): perturbation of closes/spot at minutes ≥ J and opens > J, 11 values of J, 30 cycles, V0/V1, three perturbation types (1,980 runs): orders at j ≤ J identical in all; the test has teeth (changes later orders in ~70 % of runs). Event table: 39,874 orders = unique tags; quantity exact; no tag spans >1 contract/side/day; per-role FIFO reproduced independently with 0 mismatches. Matching: no double counting; L1 ≤ L2 ≤ L3; precision/recall definitions correct; unit-lot re-implementation gives identical counts. Holdout guard works. 8/8 tests pass; start book equals the ledger in all 30 cycles after v1.1.

## D. Tests not yet written (gaps)
Causality at J > 0 and the replacement/price-stop/wing/NaN paths; the matching algorithm itself and bin-edge behaviour; classification precedence and decision-time features (nothing detects A4); the passive-lot stop issue and wing flooring; hash verification in the guard test; isolation of `fit_discovery` and the direct-`read_csv` bypass.

## E. Net effect on conclusions
1. The **scheduled-sale verdict is not robust**: ranges from fail (0.483) to pass (0.548) by convention → "timing supported, quantity unresolved".
2. **Simulated D0 P&L and the stop-cycle counts are unreliable** (A1); do not cite them as evidence for or against any rule.
3. Replacement, Clock-B and buy-back failures are **robust to every issue found** (they fail by 1–2 orders of magnitude, not by the margin of a convention).
4. Holdout independence is weaker than the pre-registration stated (constants and bypassing scripts).

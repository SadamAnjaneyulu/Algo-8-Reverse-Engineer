## Reformulations decided before running (feasibility)
* **G2, G3 and G6 as written in the audit cannot be tested exactly as phrased.** The decision-time price of an option (limit / 0.85 for a sell, limit / 1.15 for a buy) exists **only for contracts that were traded**; for the strikes and minutes where nothing happened there is none. So a ranking (AUC) against "no event" cannot use decision-time prices, and a strike-choice rule cannot be evaluated against the premiums of the strikes **not** chosen at the decision second. The reformulations below measure what can be measured and say so.
* The audit's G1 is kept as stated.

## G1. Is the "pairs after 15:15" the reactive-pair rule with its gate end extended?
**Facts recorded first (descriptive):** the reactive-pair decode left "8 pairs after 15:15" unexplained; in the late-sale work there are exactly 8 CE+PE bursts at or after 15:15 (6 post-auction, 2 pre-auction at 15:25:42 and 15:28:44). The post-auction six lie between 15:28:17 and 15:30:00, on the 15:30 candle boundary. Only 2 of the 6 bursts are same-strike pairs, so the strict reactive entry (a same-strike pair one step beyond ATM) is not what the bursts look like.
**Hypothesis H-G1:** the bursts are fires of the *unchanged* two-candle breakout rule when its gate end is not 15:15 but the end of the data (the same kind of regime shift that moved the lump clock and the D0 clearance).
**Variants:** V0 = engine rule (gate end 15:15, baseline). V1 = identical rule with gate end = last minute of data (pre-auction index to 15:29; post-auction clean futures `bt3u` to 15:39). No new parameter.
**Test:** a burst is **matched** if a V1 fire (either side) lies within +/-1 minute of the burst's start. Null: the 8 bursts' start times shifted circularly inside [15:16, end of data] within their cycle, 5,000 draws; one-sided p = P(null matches >= observed).
**Success:** matched >= 5 of 8 **and** p <= 0.05. **Descriptive:** the number of V1 fires after 15:15 and their precision against *any* real late open-short within +/-1 minute; side of the break vs the side(s) sold; strike offset of the burst legs vs "one step beyond ATM on the break side"; order-level recall of all 176 late open-short orders by V0, V1 and the frozen ladder, alone and together.
**Decision:** if supported, the pair bursts leave the late-sale problem and the reactive-pair rule's gate gets an era-dependent end (to be stated as such, one parameter: the end); if not supported, the bursts stay unexplained and the gate end 15:15 stands.

## G2. How large is the true residual of the late sales? (accounting, no new rule)
Classify every real late open-short order (176: 148 post-auction + 28 pre-auction, >= 15:15) as matched by the frozen ladder (+/-1 min, same side), matched by the V1 breakout (G1, +/-1 min, any side), both, or neither. Report counts overall, by cycle and by 5-minute block, with tolerance +/-1, +/-2, +/-5 minutes as a sensitivity table. **No success criterion; no rule is fitted.** Reformulated from the audit ("with decision-time prices") because the underlying's decision-time price is not in the log (only option limits are).

## G3. Is the 0.31 exact strike agreement in the last 25 minutes a data-lag artefact? (diagnostic)
For every real late open-short order that has a previous-minute chain: decision price d = limit / 0.85; chosen contract's previous-minute close c; drift delta = |d / c - 1|; adjacent-strike gap g = the smaller relative difference between c and the previous-minute close of the two neighbouring strikes. An order is **low-lag** if delta < g / 2 (the lag alone could not change which strike is nearest in premium to anything). Statistics: (a) the share of low-lag orders; (b) the exact-strike agreement of the **engine rule** (premium-nearest-last-sale, +/-4 steps) in the low-lag subset versus the high-lag subset, difference with a cycle-cluster bootstrap CI (4,000 draws).
**Decision:** *data-limited* if the low-lag subset has exact agreement >= 0.55 **and** the difference is >= +0.15 with the CI above 0; *rule incomplete* if the low-lag subset agreement is < 0.45 (a better price would not rescue it); otherwise *inconclusive*. No new strike rule is proposed in this step.

## G4. What are the body-module long closes, and do the two sell-to-close exceptions belong to them? (classification)
For every `num` (body module) `close_long` order on D-1 in the 32 cycles, record: whether the contract was bought by the body stream in the first minutes (opening-wing contract), the time of day, whether a buy-back occurred within 10 s (any right), whether a short-sale burst is within 60 s, exit / entry price ratio. Then classify the two exceptions (NIFTY 3 Aug 15:29:05-07, SENSEX 9 Sep 15:23 and 15:29). **Success:** both exceptions are assigned to a class defined above by the rule "opening-wing contract **and** within 10 s of a buy-back **or** within 60 s of a burst" (state which); otherwise they are reported as unclassified. Also report how often this class occurs in the last 30 minutes. No rule for *when* the opening wings are closed is fitted (it was tried in `D1_ACCURACY_PUSH` and was neutral).

## G5. Pin the cycle-stop constant with limit-implied prices (consistency check)
For each cycle with an `s_` module (the liquidation), take the second of the first `s_` order. Compute the cumulative cycle MTM (D-1 + D0, all modules, as in `bl_d0x_stop.py`) at that second under three marks: (a) previous minute close, (b) current minute open, (c) previous close for all contracts **except** the contracts the `s_` orders trade, which use their decision prices (limit / 0.85 for sells, limit / 1.15 for buys). Report the 11-12 stop levels under each mark, excluding stops that fire before 09:16 (gap at the open; listed separately). Compare with the minimum minute-close MTM of every non-stop cycle.
**Decision:** a single constant is *consistent with the data* if, under mark (c), the non-gap stop levels lie within +/-10% of their median **and** every non-stop cycle's minimum stays above the smallest stop level; otherwise *not a single MTM constant* (the stop may use another base such as capital, or scope). One scalar is estimated (the median), leave-one-cycle-out.

## G6. D0 residual buy-back classes: does the decision price reveal a threshold? (characterisation)
For D0 body buy-backs in the three residual classes (carried-lot buy-backs, "other", burst members) compute the **decision price** ratio to the lot's own entry price (D-1 entry for carried lots) and report the distribution (median, IQR, share in loss). Compare its dispersion with the **known** D0 stop class (in-loss lots, rule 1.4x on the minute high, `D0_BUYBACKS`): a class has a **price-threshold candidate** if its IQR of the decision ratio is <= 1.5x that of the known stop class **and** its median is >= 1.2. Otherwise **no price structure**. The 0.85 AUC gate of the earlier plans cannot be evaluated (no decision price for non-events); it is stated as not testable.

## Addendum 1 (written after the G1 result and before G1b ran)
**G1 result (recorded):** not supported. V1 (gate end extended) matched 0 of 8 pair bursts (null mean 0.54); V0 matched 0; V1 fires after 15:15: 11 in 29 cycles; order-level recall of the 42 pair-burst orders: ladder 26%, V1 0%. Descriptive: 5 of the 8 bursts have both legs at the same offset of +/-1 strike from ATM (the reactive-pair strike pattern); the bursts start at 15:25-15:30 (minutes 370-375).
**G1b (one follow-up, one hypothesis, no parameter):** the closing pairs are triggered at the **end of the 15:00-15:30 candle** when the candle's close (the close of the last minute before 15:30, minute index 374) lies outside the channel (highest high / lowest low) of the previous two completed candles (14:00-14:30 and 14:30-15:00), on either side. Series as in G1 (pre-auction index; post-auction clean futures). Statistic: across all cycles (those with data to minute 374), a 2x2 table (condition holds / not) x (a pair burst starting at minute >= 368 / none), Fisher exact (one-sided). **Success:** the condition holds in >= 5 of the 8 burst cycles **and** one-sided Fisher p <= 0.05. If supported, a candle-end close rule for the last candle is a candidate for the register (with the era question of the 15:30 boundary left open); if not, the bursts are carried as unexplained. No other variant (high/low based, other candles, other minutes) will be tried under this plan.

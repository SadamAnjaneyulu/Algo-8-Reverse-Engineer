# How the paired (clock) sells and the closes work — plan, written before any new test

**Questions.** (1) Are the 15-minute straddle sales unconditional (a fixed schedule), or does each slot check conditions (loss limit, strike move, premium move, delta/Greeks)? (2) Why do they sell again after closing positions mid-session — is selling linked to closes or limits? (3) How is the strike for a new sale chosen: spot ATM, or a delta/Greek/premium rule? (4) What triggers the closes (buy-backs) — a limit on loss, premium move, strike move or delta?

**Tests (D-1 primary, D0 secondary; all leave-one-cycle-out where a model is fitted; null = permuted copy of the feature inside cycle-right).**
A. *Slot decisions.* Unit = (cycle, slot k = 09:16 + 15 k, right). Outcomes: executed (any paired sale in [slot − 10 s, slot + 90 s)) and lots. Step 1: how much of the outcome the slot index alone explains (fixed schedule). Step 2: does any state variable known at the slot add information beyond the slot index (library: spot moves, book price ratios, book loss, ₹ MTM, net delta and delta-to-neutralise, gamma, vega, ATM IV and straddle price and their changes, short/long inventory, recent buy-backs, recent solo sales)? Criterion: LOCO gain in explained variance / AUC with a cluster-bootstrap CI excluding 0 **and** above the permutation-null maximum.
B. *Sell and close linkage.* Lots sold in a slot vs lots bought back since the previous slot (refill rule predicts a positive link); share of sales that follow a close within 60 s; whether skipped or reduced slots follow losses.
C. *Strike rule.* Hit rates of: nearest strike to the previous-minute spot (ATM), nearest to the forward, strike whose call delta is nearest 0.50, strike with the smallest |C − P|, strike whose straddle premium or call premium is nearest to a target share of spot. Null: same rule with a random other minute's reference.
D. *Close (buy-back) triggers per contract.* Contract-minute panel of open body shorts on D-1 (and D0): event = body buy-back of that contract in the minute. Candidates: price ÷ average entry (premium move), ₹ loss per lot, contract delta, ITM steps (strike move vs spot), spot move since the first sale of the contract, time held, gamma, time of day, whole-book net delta and loss. Hazard curves and single-variable AUC; LOCO incremental gain; a **threshold** is reported only if the hazard shows a step (rate ratio ≥ 3 between adjacent bins) that survives the permutation null.
**Decision rule.** "Conditional" requires a state variable beating the permutation null; otherwise the answer is "schedule-driven". Observational, in-sample; no trading rule recommended.

**The two sell-to-close exceptions** (NIFTY 3 Aug 15:29:05-07; SENSEX 9 Sep 15:23 and 15:29) are `num`-module long closes, not `h_` wings. G4 settled them: **SENSEX 9 Sep belongs to the opening-wing class** (within 10 s of buy-backs); **NIFTY 3 Aug does not** (a body-module sale of a contract bought by the lump at 15:06:47) and stays unexplained.

## 8. Evidence-quality issues that apply to everything above

1. **Design on the validation data.** All structure was found after viewing all 32 cycles; the "unseen" splits are inside the same log. Every accuracy figure is optimistic for new data. The binding anti-overfitting protocol (RECONSTRUCTION_GAP section 5: fit decisions not P&L, teacher-forced scoring, leave-one-cycle-out constants, one new parameter per rule, forward log locked as hold-out) still applies and was followed in the late-sale work.
2. **Contemporaneous features.** The frozen late-sale score loses its edge over a clock baseline when features are taken strictly before the minute (lag-1 ΔAUC -0.028; OVERFITTING_AUDIT). Treat the late-sale directional score as a hypothesis.
3. **Stop flips decide P&L.** Each cycle-stop flip is worth Rs 4-7 lakh; P&L comparisons on 19 cycles are mostly flip counting. Decisions, not P&L, are the target.
4. **Selection.** About 126 variants and 315 tests were counted across the late-sale rule development (EXPERIMENT_LEDGER); BH 10% applies within families, but the families share the same cycles. Separately, the pre-paper battery's PBO of 0.60 was uninformative against a noise null of 0.53 (OVERFITTING_AUDIT).
5. **Regime limits.** Two versions, one auction era change; the 7 Dec 2026 revision has no real log.
6. **Data limits.** Fills only; traded contracts only; one-minute bars; no ticks, decision logs, configuration, margin or allocation ids.

## 9. Closure standard (decided) and the G1-G6 results

### 9.0 Decision recorded
**Standard A**, confirmed by the user on 2026-11-25 **with one qualification: completing G1-G6 does not by itself make the reconstruction independently validated.** The user's five steps: (1) finish G1-G6 with pre-registered, existing-data investigations and no broad indicator search; (2) build the complete order-level simulator for D-1 **and** D0 (portfolio state, order lifecycle, stops, wings, clearance); (3) reconcile mismatches into unidentified rules, missing inputs, execution assumptions or data limits; (4) freeze the reconstruction (rules, configuration, code, test results; irreducible assumptions labelled); (5) report validation honestly: historical reproduction vs out-of-fold vs genuinely independent. If decision logs stay unavailable, state explicitly that some behaviour cannot be reconstructed from fills alone, and do not turn an unknown rule into a confirmed one to close the phase. **No original trade export after 22 Oct 2026, decision log or configuration is available** (user's earlier statement; the repository holds none), so the missing-data limitation stays explicit.

### 9.4 Closure criteria for Phase A (adopted: standard A with the user's qualification)
Phase A is closed when **all** of the following hold:
1. Every row of the register has a status; each UNRESOLVED / UNKNOWN row is either given an accepted rule or **declared irreducible** with the data that would reopen it. *(In progress: G1-G6 done.)*
2. G1-G6 are run, pre-registered, decisions not P&L, results recorded in the register. **Done 2026-11-25.**
3. An end-to-end D-1 **and** D0 simulator exists that starts from market data only and reproduces the real order sequence, reported at order level under the frozen **scoring specification** (`ORDER_LEVEL_SCORING_SPEC.md`, version 1.0, hash in `outputs/scoring/spec_freeze.json`: timestamps by tier, instrument and side, strike, quantity, order type, portfolio state after each matched event, missing and extra orders, per-cycle distributions), with its error budget. *(In progress: spec v1.1 frozen; scorer built and verified (31 tests; legacy bridge reproduces 0.6878 and 0.4103); engine now emits validity explicitly; **first D-1 order-level run done (mode F, diagnostic, historical reproduction): keys F1 0.736 at 60 s, full match 0.457; mismatch profile in `D1_ORDER_LEVEL_FIRST_RUN.md`**. Open: TF-E output, the standalone D0 engine, D0 and end-to-end order-level scoring.)*
4. Mismatches are reconciled into unidentified rules, missing inputs, execution assumptions or data limits. *(Open.)*
5. The reconstruction is **frozen with hashes** (rules, configuration, code, test results) before any hold-out is opened. *(Open.)*
6. Validation is reported in three separate labels: **historical reproduction**, **out-of-fold**, **independent** (only possible with new real cycles or the forward log; until then the third label is empty and stated as empty).

**Not** a criterion: matching real P&L (dominated by stop flips; cannot be tuned without overfitting).

## 10. Implications for the next phases (for planning only)

* **Phase B has a data problem, not a method problem.** Without new real cycles, "validation" is a consistency check on the development cycles. The audit recommends stating that explicitly rather than reporting in-sample accuracy as validation.
* **Reconstruction accuracy is not strategy profitability.** The pre-paper battery gives NO-GO (5 of 8 gates: PBO 0.60, noise floor, margin cap fail); the simulator loses money post-auction while the real log is positive. Phase B should report these separately, as you proposed.
* **Phase C inherits the unknowns.** Anything marked UNRESOLVED or irreducible enters an OMS as a configurable parameter with a recorded source, never as a hard-coded rule.

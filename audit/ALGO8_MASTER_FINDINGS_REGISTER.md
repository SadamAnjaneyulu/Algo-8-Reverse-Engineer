## How to read this register

**Common data scope (unless stated otherwise):** broker export `Trades_GKOHNP140.csv` — 41,421 fill rows,
**39,874 orders** (keyed by column 163 / `UniqueOrderIdentifier`), 35 underlying-expiry cycles
(18 NIFTY, 17 SENSEX), sessions 22/23 June – 22 October 2026; Breeze 1-minute data — 170 index-days / 63,678 bars and
3,243 traded option contract-days / 1,230,065 candles (traded contracts only, not the full chain).

**Sample type.** Every observation in June–October 2026 has been inspected by at least one lineage. Nothing in this
register is independent out-of-sample evidence. Where a study used a chronological split, the column says so:
- *in-sample*: fitted or measured on all cycles;
- *later check*: rule chosen on earlier cycles, scored on later cycles that had already been looked at;
- *DEV/VAL/TEST*: the exit-engine split (DEV ≤ 20 Aug: 18 cycles; VAL ≤ 22 Sep: 8; TEST ≥ 23 Sep: 9). TEST is untouched
  by the models and thresholds fitted in the exit engine, but **contaminated** for hand-derived constants (1.45–1.55×,
  clock windows, salvage thresholds) found earlier on all 35 cycles.

**Evidence classes** (as defined in the audit request):
**CONFIRMED** · **STRONG EVIDENCE** · **PARTIALLY UNDERSTOOD** · **HYPOTHESIS** · **UNKNOWN** · **CONTRADICTED / INVALIDATED**.
"CONFIRMED" here always means *confirmed as an observed property of this export and market data*, not confirmed
original source code.

**Source links.**
- Lineage Y (this repository branch; PRs #2 and #3): relative links, e.g. [`FINDINGS.md`](../FINDINGS.md).
